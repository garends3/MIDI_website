Research

Our research

At MiDI (Microstructure and Dynamics Imaging) Lab, we aim to understand the microstructure of biological tissues through advanced imaging. Our research integrates diffusion MRI, microscopy and computational modelling to explore tissue architecture, brain connectivity and innovative imaging methodology.

01

Diffusion MRI with plug-and-play gradient inserts

We advance diffusion MRI acquisition using flexible plug-and-play gradient inserts. Stronger and faster magnetic field gradients enable higher-resolution imaging, better sensitivity to microstructural changes and shorter acquisition times, making advanced diffusion MRI more accessible and adaptable for a wide range of studies.

We have dedicated gradient systems for head and breast imaging.

02

Unravelling microstructure with diffusion MRI and microscopy

We combine diffusion MRI with microscopy to better understand tissue microstructure. This multi-modal approach lets us validate MRI measurements against microscopic ground truth, study cellular organisation, and develop new models that relate MRI signals to microstructural features, bridging the gap between imaging and histology.

With ultra-high-field MRI at 28.2 T we image organoids and small tissue samples at very high resolution. Read about our VIDI project.

03

Flow MRI and intracranial aneurysms

Unruptured intracranial aneurysms are common, and deciding which ones need treatment is difficult. We develop deep-learning segmentation of intracranial arteries and aneurysms across CTA, MRA and 4D Flow MRI, quantify aneurysm morphology, and analyse hemodynamics with 4D Flow MRI.

Within the Aneurysm@Risk project of the SHERPA consortium, this work contributes to an image-based prediction model for aneurysm growth and rupture, using longitudinal data from several European centres.
