Paula del Popolo

PhD Student

Brief Description

Medical imaging researcher focused on the physics and modelling of diffusion MRI for probing tissue microstructure. Currently a second-year PhD student in Medical Imaging working on forward modelling diffusion MRI. Previous work includes simulation-based inference (neural posterior estimation) and optimal measurement selection for joint diffusion–relaxation MRI.

Background

BSc in Bioengineering, MSc in Medical Imaging (Utrecht University).

Keywords

Diffusion MRI, physics-informed machine learning, tissue microstructure, sequence optimisation
