New Challenge Launched: Multimodal Axon & Cell Size Estimation (ACE)

MiDI Lab is launching ACE, a community challenge bridging ultra-high-field MRI and microscopy to validate models of axon and cell size.

We invite the community to help shape it, apply your own approach, and compare it against microscopy ground truth.
